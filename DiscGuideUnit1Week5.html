Unit 1: Energy Transfer • Week 5

Day 1: Phenomenon

The Rubber Band Archer

🔍 Look Closely

A rubber band is stretched back on a launcher. It isn't moving, and it isn't making a sound. But as soon as it is released, it flies across the room hitting a target. Where was the energy while it was just sitting there stretched out?

Day 2: Investigation

Storing Energy

Using a ball and a ramp, explore how the height of an object changes its stored energy.

If you drop a ball from 10 inches versus 50 inches, which one has more energy when it hits the floor? How do you know?

💡 Stored Energy

Energy can be stored in an object's position (height) or its shape (stretched rubber band). This is called Potential Energy, though we often just call it "stored energy."

Day 4: Engineering

The Insulator Design

We want to keep hot cocoa warm for a long time. This means we need to slow down the transfer of heat energy.

Which material would make the best insulator for a cup? (Bubble wrap, Aluminum foil, or Paper). Why?

💡 Conduction Check

Insulators are materials that do NOT allow energy to transfer easily. Metals are conductors (easy transfer), while plastic and air are insulators (slow transfer).
