PLAY TO

For teachers: how the DOK levels and scoring work
DOK 1
Shown to students as Level 1 · Find It. Recall. The passage states the meaning directly with a definition or synonym clue (“or very large,” “which means,” “in other words”).
DOK 2
Shown to students as Level 2 · Figure It Out. Skill & Concept. Students infer the meaning from examples, an antonym, or cause and effect in the passage.
DOK 3
Shown to students as Level 3 · Clue Detective. Strategic Thinking. Students combine clues across several sentences, often to pick the right meaning of a multiple-meaning word.

Each round picks DOK 1, 2, or 3 at random from 60 words (20 per level). Words don’t repeat until a level runs out.

2 Players: finishing the word earns 1 point, and choosing the right meaning earns 1 more, so a word is worth up to 2 points. If the speller misses the meaning, they keep their spelling point and the other player gets one chance to steal the meaning point. If both players pass the goal with the same score, the next word breaks the tie. 1 Player: a correct meaning earns the point.
