Land the comet on the nearest ten, hundred or thousand.

Every comet carries a three-digit number. Read which place you are rounding to, choose the pad it belongs on — then watch it fly to its real spot on the number line and find out if you were right.

Flight rules

  1. Read the banner: nearest ten, hundred or thousand?
  2. Find the digit in that place — it is underlined in teal.
  3. Look at the decider, the digit to its right, glowing gold.
  4. Decider is 0–4 → stay where you are. Decider is 5–9 → climb to the next one.
  5. Every digit after the underlined one becomes 0.
