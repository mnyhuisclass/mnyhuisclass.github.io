Day 1: Phenomenon

The Mystery Circuit

🔍 Look Closely

A battery is connected to a lightbulb with two wires. When the loop is closed, the bulb glows. If we touch the bulb, it feels warm. Where did that heat come from?

Day 2: Investigation

Heat on the Move

Day 4: Application

Energy Converters

A toaster takes electrical energy and turns it into heat. A flashlight turns electrical energy into light.

Draw or describe a device that uses electrical energy to create **MOTION** (like a fan or a toy car).

💡 Energy Change

Energy is never "lost," it just changes forms! In a circuit, electrical energy often changes into Heat, Light, or Motion.
