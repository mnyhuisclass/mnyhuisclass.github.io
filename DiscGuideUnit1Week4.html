Day 1: Phenomenon

Power from Above

🔍 Look Closely

A small solar-powered toy grasshopper sits on a desk. When a bright flashlight shines on its back, it starts to buzz and jump. When the light is turned off, it stops immediately. What is moving the grasshopper?

Day 4: Engineering

The Clean Energy Challenge

Renewable resources like sun, wind, and water can be used forever without running out. Fossil fuels (like coal) are non-renewable.

Imagine you are an engineer. Indiana has many flat fields. Which renewable energy source would be BEST for a farm in Indiana? Why?

💡 Human Impact

Using renewable energy helps protect our environment because it doesn't release as much pollution into the air or water.
