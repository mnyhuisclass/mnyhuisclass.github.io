For teachers

4.CA.5 Solve real-world problems with whole numbers involving multiplicative comparison (e.g., by using drawings and equations with a symbol for the unknown number to represent the problem), distinguishing multiplicative comparison from additive comparison. Division problems never have remainders.

  • Find the bigger amount: “4 times as many as 6” (n = 4 × 6)
  • Find the smaller amount: “24 is 4 times as many as…” (4 × n = 24)
  • Find how many times: 24 is how many times as many as 6? (6 × n = 24)
  • Additive contrast: “5 more than” and “5 fewer than” problems
  • Choose the equation and multiplicative or additive? sorting items

Wrong answers are written from common mix-ups, like adding when the problem says “times as many.” Each one gets a bar-model drawing and an explanation, then a fresh problem of the same kind. The zone report at the end lists every skill and every missed problem.
