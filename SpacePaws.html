HOW TO PLAY

The Word Eater roams Planet Lexi looking for pods to raid. Each pod holds a word. Walk into a pod and answer the question to save its word.

When the alarm sounds, the Word Eater is attacking a pod. Watch the map at the bottom of the screen and hurry there before the timer runs out! Go inside and answer questions to blast it away.

Watch out for the little Nibblers on the path. Jump over them or zap them with word stars.

Once every pod is done, a BOSS appears! Jump and throw word stars at it until it gets dizzy. Then answer a question. Each correct answer is a big hit, and 5 big hits destroy the boss and clear the level.
