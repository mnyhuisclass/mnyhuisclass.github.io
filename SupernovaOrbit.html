SupernovaOrbit

Solve to fly. Buy planets. Build the richest galaxy!

About 2 to 3 minutes per round with 4 teams.
How to play
  • There are no dice. On your turn, pick a thruster and solve its problem to fly. Level 1 Commutative moves 2 spaces, Level 2 Associative moves 3, and Level 3 Distributive moves 5. Each button shows where you will land, so choose wisely!
  • Wrong answer? Your ship stays put, and you see the correct steps.
  • Land on an open planet to buy it. Other players pay you rent when they land there. Own all 3 planets in a system and rent doubles.
  • Comets: name the property to catch 50 stardust. Solar Flares cost 50, which goes to the Cosmic Fund. Dock at the Space Station to collect the whole fund.
  • The Wormhole sends you to the Asteroid Belt. Solve a Distributive problem next turn to escape and fly right away.
  • Pass the Launch Pad to collect 100. After the last round, the highest total (stardust plus planet values) wins.
